Dark-mode readability update

Install: Quit Needed Tools. Unzip this package and replace your existing app with Needed Tools.app. Reopen it and switch to dark mode. Existing vault files remain in their current folder.

The shared dark theme now gives secondary labels and captions explicit light colours across Home, Vault, Design, Shots, Sort, Grab, Credit, Pay and project/settings UI. Literal grey foreground text is also brightened separately from surfaces. Placeholders no longer have a second opacity reduction. Grab & Go, selected segmented filters and GIF badges have explicit foreground/background pairs. Disabled text controls retain a visible disabled distinction with less fading.

Light mode rules are unchanged. Existing treatment canvas and media/swatch exclusions are retained. Direct Wi-Fi transfer and the prior app fixes are included; see Direct WiFi Setup.md.

Checks: shared text-token pairs measured at least 4.92:1 against the three sampled dark surfaces; tests passed for literal greys, translucent text, selected buttons, badges, disabled versus hidden controls, and treatment exclusions. Previous dark-mode regression checks passed. App signature verified. A full visual review of every screen in the running Mac app has not been performed.
