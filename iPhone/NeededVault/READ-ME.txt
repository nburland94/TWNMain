NEEDED MOBILE VAULT FOR IPHONE
==============================

The real iPhone app, in the approved design: the project on the left, the mark in
the middle, ＋ on the right; Home, Projects and each project's own page; Grab & Go in
the middle of the glass tab bar, with the orange lens sliding between Home and
Projects.

What the app does that the web page can't: it works with no signal at all, and it
sends to your Mac by itself.

  - Grab anywhere — Camera, From Photos, the Share Sheet (in every app), Grab & Go,
    the side button. Everything is kept on the phone first.
  - When the phone is on the same Wi-Fi as your Mac (with Needed Tools open and
    Home › Your phone switched on), everything waiting goes straight into the
    right project on the Mac, with its tags. It also tries in the background now
    and then, and every time you open the app or come back onto Wi-Fi.
  - A grab counts as sent only when the Mac says it saved it. Each carries its own
    receipt number, so nothing lands twice. An orange dot = not sent yet.
  - Nothing on the phone is deleted on its own. Delete one yourself from its view
    (the Mac keeps its copy).
  - Wi-Fi only: it never sends over mobile data, and nothing goes anywhere but your
    own Mac.

PUT IT ON YOUR PHONE
  1. You need Xcode (free, App Store) and an Apple Developer account
     ($99/year — so the app stays on your phone and can go on TestFlight).
     A free Apple ID works too, but the app stops opening after 7 days until
     you press ▶ in Xcode again.
  2. Double-click "Make-Needed-Vault-iPhone.command". It asks for your Team ID
     once (or press Return and choose the team in Xcode), makes the project,
     and opens Xcode.
  3. Plug in your iPhone, pick it at the top of Xcode, press ▶.
     The first time: on the iPhone, Settings › General › VPN & Device
     Management › trust your developer account; and turn on Developer Mode
     (Settings › Privacy & Security) if it asks.

FIRST OPEN
  - It asks to pair: on the Mac, Needed Tools › Home › Your phone (switch it on),
    and point the phone at the code. Allow Camera (to read the code) and Local
    Network (to reach your Mac). Your projects come across.
  - No Mac nearby? Close the pairing screen and grab anyway — pair later from the
    mark at the top (How it works › Pair).

THE SIDE BUTTON AND BACK TAP
  - Settings › Action Button › Shortcut › Mobile Vault › "Grab & Go".
  - Or Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap › Double Tap › "Grab & Go",
    and Triple Tap › "Save my latest screenshot".
  - Siri: "Grab and Go in Mobile Vault".

IF IT WON'T SEND
  - Same Wi-Fi as the Mac? Needed Tools open, Home › Your phone switched on?
  - iPhone Settings › Mobile Vault › Local Network must be on.
  - Some networks (hotels, offices) keep devices apart — try your home Wi-Fi.
  - "Your Mac has a new code": tap the mark › Scan again.

UPDATING FROM THE OLD APP
  The old app (round 16) saved into an iCloud folder; those files are still there
  and Needed Tools' Sync still reads them. The new app starts fresh on the phone.
