NEEDED VAULT FOR IPHONE
=======================

The iPhone app for your vault — the mock-up, for real. Browse a project's
stills, clips and GIFs; tap one for the dark full view; Photos and Screenshot
add to the project; Grab & Go sends everything straight in. "Needed Vault" is
in every app's Share Sheet, and "Grab & Go" can go on the side button.

It reads and writes the same Needed Vault folder in iCloud that Needed Tools
mirrors on your Mac (Home › Your phone › Keep it in iCloud). Nothing else to
set up on the Mac.

PUT IT ON YOUR PHONE
  1. You need Xcode (free, App Store) and an Apple Developer account
     ($99/year — so the app stays on your phone and can use TestFlight).
     A free Apple ID works too, but the app stops opening after 7 days until
     you press ▶ in Xcode again.
  2. Double-click "Make-Needed-Vault-iPhone.command". It asks for your Team ID
     once (or press Return and choose the team in Xcode), makes the project,
     and opens Xcode.
  3. Plug in your iPhone, pick it at the top of Xcode, press ▶.
     The first time: on the iPhone, Settings › General › VPN & Device
     Management › trust your developer account; and turn on Developer Mode
     (Settings › Privacy & Security) if it asks.

FIRST OPEN
  - Tap "Choose the Needed Vault folder" and pick iCloud Drive › Shortcuts ›
    Needed Vault. That's it — your projects appear.
  - The first Screenshot asks for Photos access. Photos you pick with the
    Photos button don't need it.

THE SIDE BUTTON AND BACK TAP
  - Settings › Action Button › Shortcut › Needed Vault › "Grab & Go".
  - Or Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap › Double Tap › "Grab & Go",
    and Triple Tap › "Save my latest screenshot".
  - Siri: "Grab and Go in Needed Vault".

HOW IT FITS
  - What you add goes into Needed Vault/<Project>/Inbox in iCloud. Press Sync
    on the Mac and it's filed into the project's vault, with #tags from the
    name.
  - If iCloud can't be reached, things wait on the phone and go in the next
    time the app opens ("2 wait on this phone").
  - The grid shows the project's files directly; Needed Tools' Sync also
    writes a small index (names, tags, colours, tiny previews) so it's quick.

The web page and the Shortcuts from rounds 12–14 aren't needed any more.
