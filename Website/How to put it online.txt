This Was Needed — the website, with Needed Mobile Vault at /vault/
===========================================================

What's here
  index.html                   the site (Work, Awards, About, Contact, Tools, Mobile Vault)
  this-was-needed-logo.svg     the logo, on its own
  vault/                       Needed Mobile Vault — the phone page
  _headers                     tells Netlify not to hold on to old copies of the phone page
  _redirects                   sends /Vault, /mobile-vault and similar to /vault/

Put it online (Netlify) — ONCE: link it to GitHub (needed from round 32)
  Your projects now reach your phone by themselves through a tiny "relay" on the site
  (Website/netlify/functions/pair.mjs). Netlify only runs that when the site is built from
  your GitHub repo — a drag-and-drop deploy doesn't include it. One time:
  1. Netlify › the thiswasneeded.info site › Site configuration › Build & deploy ›
     Link repository › GitHub › nburland94/TWNMain (allow Netlify access if it asks).
  2. Branch to deploy: the branch Claude pushes to (or main once it's merged).
     Base directory: Website   Build command: (leave empty)   Publish directory: Website
     (Website/netlify.toml fills in the rest, and package.json brings the storage it uses.)
  3. Deploy. From then on every push updates the site on its own — no more dragging folders.
  Check: open https://thiswasneeded.info/api/pair?id=0000000000000000 — it should say
  {"error":"not yet"}. That means the relay is live.

Or, the old way (Netlify drag and drop) — works, but new projects won't reach the phone
  1. Unzip. Open the folder: you should see index.html, vault, assets, _headers, _redirects
     right there at the top. Drag THAT folder (the one those sit in) onto
     Netlify › the site thiswasneeded.info uses › Deploys › the drop area.
     (If the site is linked to a folder or a Git repo, replace its files with these.)
     Check: in Deploys, the new deploy says "Published", and its file list shows vault/ at the top.
  2. Your domain (thiswasneeded.info) should point at that Netlify site. Netlify gives it
     https on its own — Needed Mobile Vault needs https to work offline.
  3. Open https://thiswasneeded.info/vault/ on your iPhone to check.

Needed Tools makes its QR code for https://thiswasneeded.info/vault/. If the site ends up
somewhere else, tell me the address and I'll point the app at it.

What the website holds
  Only the pages themselves (a few hundred KB). No photos, GIFs or clips are ever sent to the
  website — they stay on the phone until you tap Sync, which saves them into your own Photos;
  iCloud Photos carries them to your Mac. Nothing to store, no server, no accounts.

Needed Tools stays "Coming soon" on the Tools section until you're ready to sell it.
