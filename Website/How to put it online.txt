This Was Needed — the website, with Needed Vault at /vault/
===========================================================

What's here
  index.html                   the site (Work, Awards, About, Tools, Contact, Vault)
  this-was-needed-logo.svg     the logo, on its own
  vault/                       Needed Vault — the phone page
  _headers                     tells Netlify not to hold on to old copies of the phone page

Put it online (Netlify)
  1. Netlify › your site › Deploys › drag this whole "Website" folder onto the drop area.
     (Or, if the site is linked to a folder or a Git repo, replace its files with these.)
  2. Your domain (thiswasneeded.info) should point at that Netlify site. Netlify gives it
     https on its own — Needed Vault needs https to work offline.
  3. Open https://thiswasneeded.info/vault/ on your iPhone to check.

Needed Tools makes its QR code for https://thiswasneeded.info/vault/. If the site ends up
somewhere else, tell me the address and I'll point the app at it.

What the website holds
  Only the pages themselves (a few hundred KB). No photos, GIFs or clips are ever sent to the
  website — they stay on the phone until you tap Sync, which saves them into your own Photos;
  iCloud Photos carries them to your Mac. Nothing to store, no server, no accounts.

Needed Tools stays "Coming soon" on the Tools section until you're ready to sell it.
