How it gets to your Mac

  1. Tap Sync, then Save Images (or Save Video). They go into this phone’s Photos.
  2. iCloud Photos takes them to your Mac — anywhere you’ve got signal.
  3. On the Mac, press Sync in Needed Tools. Each goes into its project’s vault, with its tags, and into Needed Vault › its project in Photos.

Next to your Mac? You can pick it under AirDrop instead. Either way, nothing lands in the vault twice.
