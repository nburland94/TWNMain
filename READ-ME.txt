NEEDED TOOLS — BUILD KIT (Rounds A, B and C)

All six tools in one app: Grab, Vault, Shots, Sort, Credit and Pay.

BUILDING
  Double-click "Build-Needed-Tools". Allow it once in System Settings →
  Privacy & Security if macOS blocks it. It compiles, signs and opens
  Needed Tools.

  If macOS says the kit "is damaged and can't be opened": it isn't — it's
  the download flag macOS puts on anything from the web. Press Cancel,
  open Terminal, type  bash  and a space, drag Build-Needed-Tools into
  the window, and press Return. The script clears the flag from the kit
  and builds as normal.

  This is the first time six apps' code has been compiled together, so
  it may stop with errors. If it does, a file called build-errors.txt
  appears beside the script — drop it into the chat and I'll fix it.

WHAT'S IN ROUND A
  • Sign-in (placeholder) — press Sign in; nothing's checked yet.
  • The top bar: Home and the six tools as tabs (⌘1 to ⌘7), your vault,
    the project (orange), and Grab & Go.
  • One vault and one project for everything. Change them in the top
    bar or inside any tool — every tool follows.
  • Each tool keeps its work while you switch tabs. Tools open the first
    time you visit them, so launch stays quick.
  • Home (Round B):
      – The six tools along the top.
      – Drop anything onto Home from Finder: a film opens in Grab, a
        card or folder in Sort, a call sheet (PDF or text) in Credit,
        and images save straight into the project's Stills.
      – The vault wall: the project's newest stills, GIFs and clips in
        their real shapes. Click one to open it in the Vault. An empty
        project shows a big drop zone instead.
      – Focus timer: ± in fives, Start, Pause, Resume, Reset. macOS
        tells you when it's done, even if you're in another app —
        allow notifications the first time.
      – Calendar: your week from macOS Calendar, which includes Google
        calendars if your Google account is added in System Settings →
        Internet Accounts. Press "Show my calendar" and allow access.

BEFORE YOU SWITCH FROM THE SEPARATE APPS
  • Needed Credit: your contacts live inside the old app. Open the old
    Needed Credit → Crew memory → Export backup, then in Needed Tools →
    Credit → Import that file.
  • Pay, Vault, Shots and Sort keep everything in files and folders, so
    they carry across on their own.

ROUND C
  • Each tool keeps its title — Needed Grab, Needed Vault and so on.
  • The welcome: full glass over the app, after you sign in. Eight
    pages — Needed Tools, Grab & Go, then each tool's steps. "Don't
    show this again" keeps it away; ? in the top bar brings it back,
    opened at the tool you're in.
  • Hints: the first time you open a tool, glass hints point at its
    main controls, one at a time. "Got it" moves on; "All the steps"
    opens that tool's page of the welcome.
  • Undocking: the small arrow beside the open tool's tab moves it into
    its own window — nothing's lost. Press Dock in that window's title
    bar, or close it, and it goes back into its tab.

ROUND D
  • Drop anything works: files from Finder, and images dragged straight
    from a browser.
  • Focus timer: at zero, a chime and "Time's up" over Home, with Reset
    or Five more. macOS notifies you too.
  • ← and → in the top bar. ← closes whatever's open first (a reference,
    a pop-up), then goes back a tab. Clicking the blur around a pop-up
    closes it too.
  • Every tool on the apricot glow, and filling the window at any size.
  • A sleeker sign-in. The welcome in Home's light glass, with Grab & Go
    as the last page.

ROUND E — HOME
  • Above the wall: References · Grabs · Both, and Entire vault · This
    project. Your choice is remembered.
    (References are what the Vault saved; grabs are what Needed Grab —
    or you, in Finder — put in a project.)
  • The wall: tighter, scrolls for more, and GIFs play.
  • Click anything: it floats over a blurred Home, no frame, with its
    details and options in a glass panel — Open in Vault, Open the
    source, Show in Finder, ← →. Click outside, Esc or ← to close.
  • Drop images on Home (from Finder or a browser): a pop-up asks which
    project, and whether they're references or grabs.

ROUND F
  • Folders, for everything new (your existing files stay where they are):
        Project/Grabs/       Stills · GIFs · Motion   ← Needed Grab
        Project/References/  Stills · GIFs · Motion   ← Needed Vault
    Images dropped on Home go to whichever you pick in the pop-up. The
    Vault, Home and Shots look in the old folders and both new ones.
  • Needed Shots: Shot list · Scene breakdown. Breakdown hides the
    dropdowns — one free line per beat — and the PDF follows. Flip back

  • Calendar: Week · Month (remembered), ‹ › and Today, click any day

OVERNIGHT

    Credit → Pay (tabs, Home, the welcome, ⌘2–⌘7).
  • Home: quick actions (Paste a link · New shot list · Scan a card ·
    Open a film · New invoice) in place of Drop anything. Clips play on
    the wall. Drag pictures to reorder — your order is kept. The viewer
    shows the picture instantly and never a broken icon; Move to Trash.
  • Faster: Home only re-reads the vault when it has changed, and
    remembers each picture's shape.
  • Grab & Go names files by what they really are (AVIF, WebP…), and
    the vault serves files by their real type — so mislabelled ones open.
  • Each tool shows its own intro the first time you open it.
  • The Vault refreshes every time you open its tab; clips without a
    preview frame show the clip itself.
  • Grab and the Vault: a progress bar while a film comes in; players
    in the film's own shape; "Shape of the GIF or MP4". The Vault's
    viewer floats frameless over the blur.
  • Shots: references stay in Scene breakdown, and print.
  • Pay: optional Job number; no quantity column when every line is 1;
    Email it — a new email in Mail to the client, PDF attached.
  • A touch more contrast and gloss throughout.

SENDING INVOICES
  • Pay → Settings → Sending email: pick Gmail, Outlook, iCloud or Other,
    your address, and an app password (Gmail: Google Account → Security
    → 2-Step Verification must be on → App passwords). Save, then "Send
    me a test". The password is kept in your Mac's Keychain.
  • On an invoice, Send: it's exported if needed, then a panel with the
    client's address, subject and message filled in. Send — the PDF goes
    attached, with a copy to you — and the invoice notes when and to whom.
  • "In Mail" still opens it in Apple Mail instead, if you prefer.

    Next, Back, Got it — with a ring round the control. Steps wait for
    their controls (Sort's scene breaks appear after you scan).
    ✕ closes it for now; "Don't show again" stops it; ? replays it.
  • Home: the References/Grabs and vault/project switches and the quick
    actions share one line; narrow windows show the actions as icons.
  • Vault: Add to vault — choose images, GIFs or clips from Finder, or
    drag them onto the window (several at once). They go into the
    project's References. A single film dropped still opens in the player.

ROUND 3 (final changes) — VAULT AND GRAB
  • Shapes for each one: every still has its own Original · 16:9 · 4:5 ·
    1:1 · 9:16, right under it — bigger buttons, dark when on. "Frame
    these shapes" chooses what's in shot. The row below the stills is what
    each new still starts with. The GIF and the MP4 each have their own
    shapes too: pick several and each saves as its own file.
  • New folder names, so Grab and the Vault never share one (Lexus is
    just the example — it's always your project's name):
        Lexus/Lexus_Grab/    Stills_Grab · GIFs_Grab · Motion_Grab   ← Grab
        Lexus/Lexus_Vault/   Stills_Ref  · GIFs_Ref  · Motion_Ref    ← Vault
        Lexus/Lexus_Vault/Mood/                                      ← mood boards
    Your existing files stay where they are and are still found.
    Moving a reference to another project renames its folder to match.
  • Sync, top right, left of your vault: looks through the project you're
    in — files from Grab or Finder, missing previews, missing colour
    palettes — then brings Home, the Vault, Shots and Grab up to date.

  • The Vault showing nothing: fixed at the root. The tools were each

  • Mood tab in the Vault: a PDF of stills and no words — from a project,

  • Project labels: Sports, Comedy, Fashion and your own, in the dropdown

  • The loading pill is on Home too now (Sync shows it), and the top bar's
    messages — Grab & Go, Sync, labels — actually show.
  • The walkthroughs and the welcome cover all of the above.

ROUND 5 — THE PROJECT PAGE
  • Project, beside the project in the top bar (or ⌘0): one page for the
    job. Its title, client, producer and director; the label and tags;
    and a countdown to the shoot.
  • Timeline: your dates, plus any calendar event with the project's name
    in it (read only — nothing is written to your calendar).
  • Mood: the project's references and grabs and the colours they share.
    It only appears once the project has some — a producer's project with
    no references never shows an empty box.
  • Deliverables (with the idea and the must-haves) and To do, with a
    progress bar. Type "Hero film 60s 16:9" and the shape and length come
    out on their own.
  • Documents: everything made here (Pay invoices, Shots lists, mood
    boards) and everything dropped in. Drop anything onto the page, or
    Home, or use Add files: it's copied in and filed by what it is —
    Lexus › Lexus_Docs › Treatments › Treatment v4.pdf. Versions are kept:
    a new treatment is the next v, never a replacement. Change its type
    and it moves folder. Pictures and films become references in the
    Vault, with their colours.
  • Read it: a brief, treatment or schedule is read on the Mac — rules on
    every Mac, and Apple's on-device model as well where macOS has it
    (macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence). It shows what it found; nothing
    is added until you tick and say so. Nothing leaves the Mac.
  • Send: pick documents, pick people (To, Cc, Bcc), subject, message,
    drafts kept with the project. It sends from the same account Pay
    sends invoices from — set up once, in Pay › Settings. A copy goes in
    Lexus_Docs › Sent and each document notes who it went to.
  • One contact book: crew from Credit, clients from Pay, and everyone
    you add on a project. Matched by email, then name; the Contact book
    button shows likely duplicates to merge.
  • Crew: add from the contact book or type a name; To book → Pencilled →
    Confirmed. Make credits opens Credit with every name, role and handle
    already in.
  • Credit now sends a notification when it's read a crew list.
  • Project names can be up to 80 characters in every tool (some stopped at 60).
  • The vault's list and the contact book each keep a copy a day for 30
    days (.vault/backups, and ~/Library/Application Support/NeededTools/backups).
