Direct Wi-Fi

Straight into your Mac’s Vault

  1. Keep your phone and Mac on the same trusted Wi-Fi. Keep Needed Tools open and the Mac awake.
  2. Choose a project and add photos or clips. Tap Send to Mac.
  3. Keep this Safari page open until each item says received by Mac. It is then in your project, with its tags.

If interrupted, originals stay queued in this browser. Reopen this same address while connected and tap Retry. Bookmark the Safari page; do not clear website data while transfers are pending. This local page needs your Mac to load and is separate from the website Vault.

Needed Mobile Vault

How it works

  1. Add photos and clips to a project on this direct page while your Mac is reachable.
  2. Tap Send to Mac to transfer over your Wi-Fi. Keep this page open until the Mac confirms receipt.
  3. The Mac files each item into its project automatically. No Photos permission or Sync button is needed.
