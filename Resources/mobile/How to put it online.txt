Needed Vault — the page for your phone

Put this folder online once, then open the link on your iPhone:
  1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop on your Mac.
  2. Drag this whole folder onto it. You get a link.
  3. Sign up (free) so the link stays for good.
  4. On your iPhone, open the link in Safari › Share › Add to Home Screen.

Or put it at thiswasneeded.info/vault — any web host takes this folder as it is.
The page keeps nothing of yours online: it only starts the Shortcuts on your phone.
